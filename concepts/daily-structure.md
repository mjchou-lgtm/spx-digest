# Daily structure: the forced-flow windows

The SPX session is not uniform. At a few points in the day a group of participants has to trade, whatever their view. Those windows are where the day's structure is set, and the three cards are timed around them. All times are ET / PT.

| Window | What happens | Who is forced |
|---|---|---|
| Open, 09:30 / 06:30 | Overnight orders, market-on-open orders and overnight hedges all clear at once. 0DTE implied vol is at its high for the day. | Funds with opening orders; market makers hedging the opening option flow; anyone carrying an overnight hedge. |
| Opening range, 09:30–10:00 / 06:30–07:00 | The first 30 minutes set the opening-range high and low. 10:00 / 07:00 releases land at the end of it. | Desks reacting to the 08:30 / 05:30 and 10:00 / 07:00 data. |
| IV crush, roughly 09:35–10:30 / 06:35–07:30 | Once the open has cleared, the uncertainty priced into the straddle bleeds out. The digest measures the straddle against its 09:35 / 06:35 print. | Option sellers marking down; holders of long premium bought before the open. |
| Classification, 10:30 / 07:30 | The digest fixes the day type from where price is against the opening range and the prior value area. | Nobody. It is a reading, not a flow. |
| Lunch, about 11:30–13:30 / 08:30–10:30 | Volume troughs and ranges compress. Europe's cash close falls at the start of it. | Few participants are forced. Moves here are less informative. |
| Theta cliff, from about 14:00 / 11:00 | Under square-root-of-time decay half of the day's ATM extrinsic value is gone by about 14:22 / 11:22, and the loss per minute keeps rising. ATM gamma rises with it. | Market makers and option sellers, whose hedges change faster per point of index move. |
| Closing imbalance, 15:50 / 12:50 | The exchanges publish closing-auction imbalances, and market-on-close orders are locked in. | Index funds, ETFs and leveraged ETFs that must trade at the close; dealers hedging those orders. |

## How the cards map to it

- **Pre-market card, 09:15 / 06:15**: the gap, the overnight range, vol and the level stack, before the open clears.
- **Mid-session card, 10:45 / 07:45**: after the opening range, the IV crush and the 10:30 / 07:30 classification. The early playbook window is 10:00–13:00 / 07:00–10:00.
- **Afternoon card, 14:15 / 11:15**: ahead of the late window, 14:30–15:45 / 11:30–12:45, which ends before the closing-imbalance flow.

About

A read-only quick-reference digest for the SPX 0DTE session: the day type, the premium picture, the level stack, and which of two playbooks is live. Three cards per trading day: pre-market 09:15 ET / 06:15 PT, mid-session 10:45 ET / 07:45 PT, afternoon 14:15 ET / 11:15 PT (on early-close days there is no mid-session card and the afternoon card moves to 60 minutes before the close). Every number, the regime label and the playbook status are computed by code; Claude writes only the verdict line and a short narrative from those fields. Nothing here places, modifies or cancels orders, and the codebase holds no brokerage credentials.

Approximations

  • Value area (POC / VAH / VAL): volume-at-price from 5-minute bars, 1-point bins, 70% value area. SPX has no volume, so each SPX bar's range is weighted by SPY 5-minute volume. Coarser than a tick-based profile.
  • VWAP: SPX typical price weighted by SPY 5-minute volume. A VWAP cross is a 5-minute close moving from one side of the running VWAP to the other.
  • Expected move: the ATM straddle (mid, or last if there is no quote) from the delayed Yahoo chain.
  • IV crush: the straddle now against its 09:35 ET print. With no print for the day it is an estimate from VIX1D times the square root of time left, and labelled as an estimate.
  • Theta reference: square-root-of-time decay of ATM extrinsic value.
  • Pre-market spot: ES last minus the ES–SPX basis at the prior cash close.
  • Dealer gamma / GEX: not computed or estimated.

Day-type rules

Labels: trend_up, trend_down, balance, gap_and_go, gap_and_fill, undetermined. The label is set from a snapshot at the classification time and the VWAP count up to its cutoff; before that, or when no rule set is fully met, it is undetermined. Thresholds live in regime_rules.yaml; the playbook settings in playbook.yaml (see rules).

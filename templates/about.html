About

A read-only macro digest for the SPX session: regime, catalysts that move premiums, and levels to watch. Three posts per trading day (05:45, 09:30, 11:45 PT; on early-close days there is no midsession post and the afternoon post moves to 60 minutes before the close). All numbers and the regime label are computed by code; Claude writes the prose from the computed brief and may not add numbers. Nothing here places, modifies or cancels orders, and the codebase holds no brokerage credentials.

Approximations

  • GEX / gamma flip: approximate, OI-based. Per strike OI × gamma × 100 × spot² × 0.01, calls positive, puts negative, gamma from Black-Scholes on the chain's IV. Assumes a customer-long-puts/short-calls book; it is not dealer positioning. The flip is where the summed profile crosses zero within ±5% of spot.
  • Expected move: ATM straddle (mid, or last if no quote). Intraday the 0DTE straddle prices only the remaining move, so gap and range ratios use the day-start value from the premarket brief.
  • VWAP: SPX has no volume; SPX typical price is weighted by SPY 5-minute volume.
  • Pre-market spot: ES last minus the ES–SPX basis at the prior cash close.

Regime rules

Each label scores the weighted share of its evaluable conditions that are met. Conditions with null inputs are skipped. A label must meet its required conditions. NO_TRADE is assigned by data-quality and market-closed gates, when no label reaches the minimum score, or when the top two are within the conflict margin at low confidence. Thresholds: regime_rules.yaml.
